Upset pickThe model picks the team the betting market has as the underdog. Ultra upset is an upset pick that is also a strong pick.
LineThe sportsbook favorite and spread, and the over/under. An orange O/U pill with Over or Under marks a game where the model’s total is 10+ points off the line.
