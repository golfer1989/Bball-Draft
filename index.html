How to read this page
Predicted scoreThe model’s projected final. The bigger number is the pick.
Edge barHow far apart the two teams rate. A full bar is a lopsided matchup.
Strong pickThe rating gap is big enough that the model has historically been right about 80% of the time.
Upset pickThe model picks the team the betting market has as the underdog. Ultra upset is an upset pick that is also a strong pick.
LineThe sportsbook favorite and spread, and the over/under. An orange O/U pill with Over or Under marks a game where the model’s total is 10+ points off the line.
BetWake Forest +17.5−110The one bet the model likes in that game: the side, spread or total where its chance beats the price. The projected winner is not always the bet; a favorite can be the pick to win and still be a bad number. Games with no Bet pill have no edge.
