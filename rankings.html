How to read the rankings

The gap between two teams’ ratings, plus about 4 points for the home team, is the projected margin when they play: a +20 team at home against a +8 team projects to win by about 16. Offense, Defense and Schedule are national ranks in the three parts a rating is built from: what a team does with the ball, what it allows, and how strong its opponents have been (1 is the toughest schedule). The list is recalculated every morning from the games played so far.

Early-season note: the typical team has played 6 games, so about 70% of a typical rating still comes from last season’s team. That share shrinks with every game until a team has played 20, so expect the list to move more than a poll does.

Ratings come from each team’s shooting, turnovers, rebounding and free throws on both ends of the floor, adjusted for the opponents it has played. They are the model’s opinion, not a poll, and they move as results come in. How the model works.
